About

I am a master's student in mathematics at Wake Forest University, working with Sarah Mason. My research interests lie in algebraic combinatorics, particularly quasisymmetric functions, tableaux, and connections to representation theory.

Previously, I studied computer science and statistics and data science at Loyola Marymount University, where I worked with Joshua Hallam.

Research

My research explores how combinatorial objects, such as words and tableaux, describe relationships between families of quasisymmetric functions. I am particularly interested in Young quasisymmetric Schur functions, dual immaculate functions, and actions of 0-Hecke algebras.

Undergraduate thesis

Towards the Signed Decomposition of Young Quasisymmetric Schur Functions into Dual Immaculate Quasisymmetric Functions

Loyola Marymount University, 2025.
Supervised by Joshua Hallam.

Teaching

I serve as a graduate teaching assistant and mathematics tutor at Wake Forest University.

I also lead undergraduate reading courses, including a reading course on Catalan numbers.
