About me

Welcome.

I am a second-year master's student in mathematics at Wake Forest University, working with Sarah Mason. My research lies in algebraic combinatorics, where I use objects such as words and tableaux to study algebraic structures.

Before coming to Wake Forest, I studied computer science and statistics and data science at Loyola Marymount University, with a minor in mathematics. There, I worked with Joshua Hallam on the relationship between Young quasisymmetric Schur functions and dual immaculate functions.

Alongside my research, I serve as a graduate teaching assistant and mathematics tutor, and I lead undergraduate reading courses.
